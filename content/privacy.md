*Last updated: October 3, 2026*

This site does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal data. If you email me, I use your address only to reply.

**Analytics.** The site uses Cloudflare's analytics to see aggregate usage, such as pages viewed and approximate location. It is not used to identify individual visitors.

**Cookies and advertising.** Once ads are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's earlier visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also read [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**External links.** Posts link to outside sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which I don't control.
