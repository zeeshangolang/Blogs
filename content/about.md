This site follows how AI is changing the job market for computer science students and new graduates: hiring data, layoffs, and what employers say they want.

Every news claim links to its original source. Drafts are prepared with AI assistance and then reviewed and edited by Zeeshan, a self-taught backend developer who adds his own commentary to each post. Zeeshan is responsible for what is published here.

If you spot an error, please use the [contact page](/contact/) and it will be corrected.
